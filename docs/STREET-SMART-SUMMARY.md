# Street Smart: One-Page Summary

**What it is.** A new opt-in section with 5 decks of short, real case studies. The goal: spot the trap and pick a safe way out.

**The 5 decks.**
1. Hard Reads: 50 short passages on hard topics (faith, politics, genocide, cults, abusers), each with 5 to 7 test-style questions. New "passage then questions" mode.
2. Street Safety: 60 real-case scenes, each with 6 clues. Tap the red flags. New multi-pick mode.
3. Legal Traps: 70 cards on California and federal law, taxes, and insurance, with real laws and cases.
4. Power Plays: 60 real public conflicts. Pick the best way out, then see a quick step chart.
5. Estate Planning: 60 cards on wills, trusts, POA, beneficiaries, and CA probate.

**Same as now.** Gut Check rounds, 3 choices, the "how sure" tap, the fix screen, Leitner boxes, ages 15 to 30 and 30+, every fact sourced, and the deck checker that stops answer giveaways.

**Safety rules.** Teach spotting and defense only. No how-to for harm, no gore, no praise of evil ideas. Faiths treated fairly. Cults described by behavior, never by naming a mainstream church. Our own tactic names, nothing copied from 48 Laws of Power. Only well-known public facts about real people, no photos. Legal cards say "not legal advice" and cite real laws. No made-up cases.

**Defaults.** Section off until turned on. Hard Reads and Power Plays set to adults only, with a parent PIN to unlock for teens.

**Build order.** Legal first (no new screens), then Street Safety, Estate, Power Plays, and Hard Reads last.

**Size.** About 550 scored items.
